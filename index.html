Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers our address copy and paste Chrome extension.

What the extension does

The extension has one purpose: copy a buyer's name and shipping address from an eBay order and paste that address into a supplier's checkout page, such as Walmart or Amazon.

Information handling

  • The extension reads the buyer name and shipping address on supported eBay pages so you can copy them with one click.
  • Address and clipboard data are handled locally on your device and are never sent to any server.
  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • A license key, if you enter one, is stored locally in Chrome's storage and checked locally.
  • The extension has no analytics, tracking, or telemetry.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share buyer or address information.

Payments

Licenses are sold through Stripe. Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy, and we never see your full card details.

Changes

We may update this policy as the extension changes. The current version is always at https://lukeprofits.github.io/address-paste-privacy/.
